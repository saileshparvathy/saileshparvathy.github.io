Welcome! I am a PhD student at the [Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy](https://lkyspp.nus.edu.sg), [National University of Singapore](https://nus.edu.sg). I study geoeconomics - how countries use economic networks as tools to attain geopolitical goals. My current work focuses on identifying the effects of geoeconomic coercion (e.g: trade and financial sanctions) using satellite imagery and granular financial data. I work under the guidance of [Prof. Ramkishen Rajan](https://lkyspp.nus.edu.sg/our-people/faculty/rajan-ramkishen-sundara).

Previously, I earned an MA in Development Studies from IIT Guwahati and worked as a pre-doctoral fellow at IIM Bangalore.  

You can read more about my work [here](/research/) and find my CV [here](/cv/).
